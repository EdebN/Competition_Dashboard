Competition Dashboard · Feature update

Player Card Studio has been discontinued.

The built-in player-card creator is no longer maintained. You can still display player cards on your team page by creating the artwork elsewhere and uploading it as an image.

Make a player card image

  1. Open the FUTBIN FC 26 Card Creator.
  2. Build the card and fill in the details you want shown.
  3. Export or download the finished card as an image to your device.
  4. Open your Team Page Editor. In the left panel, choose Image, then upload the saved card image.
  5. Resize and position the image on the page, then save and publish your changes.
Open FUTBIN Card Creator ↗ Team Workspace Competition Dashboard

For lineup displays, add a Player field in the Team Page Editor. Image-only player cards can be selected from the saved image library after the images have been added through Asset Studio.
